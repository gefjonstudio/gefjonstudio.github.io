Sign — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Sign is a document signing app that works entirely on your iPhone or iPad. In short: your documents and signatures never leave your device, and we collect no data.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Sign has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information.

2. Your documents and signatures

Documents you bring in, the signatures and initials you keep, and the signed copies you make are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are not uploaded anywhere. They are included in your device's own backups (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on; Apple handles those backups, not us.

3. Sending a signed copy

A document only leaves the app when you choose to send it, mail it, save it to Files or print it. Where it goes after that is up to you and the app or service you choose. The optional signing record page and password are added on your device.

4. Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan paper. Photo access is used only when you pick a photo to bring in as a document, to place on a page, or to take your signature from. Nothing is uploaded.

5. Purchases

The one-time purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction.

6. Children

Sign is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
