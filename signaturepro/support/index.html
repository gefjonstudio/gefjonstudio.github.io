Sign — Support

Need help with Sign?
Email: gefjonstudio@gmail.com
We usually reply within 2 business days.

Quick answers

  • Bring in a document: tap Bring in a document, then Scan paper, Files or Photos. Sign opens PDFs, photos, Word, Excel, Pages and more, and turns them into a PDF on your device. New here? Tap Sign a practice lease.
  • Sign it: drag your signature, initials or the date from Your hand onto the page, or tap the page where it goes. Drag a mark to move it; use its drop of ink to resize it. More adds text, a check, a cross, an image or logo, and Someone else signs (they sign in person on your device).
  • Finish: hold the key that says Hold to put it in ink. Your marks become part of the page, and your original is kept.
  • Send it: on the In ink screen, tap Send the signed copy, or Mail, Files or Print. The ••• button adds the signing record page or locks the copy with a password.
  • Sign it again: open the signed copy from In ink, tap •••, then Sign the original again.
  • Your saved signatures and initials: on the home screen tap ••• → Settings, then Your signatures or Your initials. Press and hold one to remove it.
  • Price: your first 3 documents are free. After that, one purchase lets you keep signing. No subscription, nothing renews.
  • Restore a purchase: on the home screen tap ••• → Settings → Restore purchase, or tap Restore purchase on the purchase screen.
  • Privacy: everything stays on your device. No account, no servers, nothing tracks you.

Privacy policy and terms of use: in Sign, tap ••• → Settings, then Privacy or Terms at the bottom.
