Liner notes: 5 things every contractor invoice needs

  1. Your business details. Name, phone, email and your licence number if your trade needs one.
  2. An invoice number and date. Number invoices in order so you and your accountant can track who has paid.
  3. Itemised labour and materials. Line items with quantities get paid faster than one lump sum.
  4. A clear due date. Due on receipt or due in 14 days. Vague terms get paid last.
  5. How to pay you. PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App or bank details, or a scan-to-pay QR code on the invoice.
