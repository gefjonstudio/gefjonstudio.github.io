Quick answers

  • New invoice or estimate: tap New Invoice or New Estimate on the Home tab, add a client and items, then tap Preview.
  • Send: tap Send to email, text, AirDrop or share the PDF. It's marked as sent automatically.
  • Customer approval: open an estimate, tap Get Signature and hand your phone to your customer. Once signed, tap Create Invoice.
  • Get paid: add PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle or bank details in Settings → How clients pay you. They print on every invoice with a QR code.
  • Record a payment: open an invoice and tap Record Payment (deposits and partial payments are supported).
  • Templates: Settings → Template & colours, or change the look of one document from the editor.
  • Pricing: everything is free to create and preview; your first 3 sends are free. Invoice Once Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription.
  • Restore a purchase: Settings → Unlock Pro → Restore Purchase.
  • Privacy: everything stays on your device. No account, no servers.
