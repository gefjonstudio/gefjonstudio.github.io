Invoice Once — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Invoice Once is an invoice and estimate maker that works entirely on your device. In short: your business data stays on your iPhone or iPad, and we collect nothing.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Invoice Once has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information.

2. Your business data

Your business details, logo, clients, saved items, invoices, estimates, payments, job photos and signatures are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are not uploaded anywhere. They are included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on; that backup is controlled by Apple and you.

3. Sending documents

A document only leaves the app when you choose to send it by email, message, AirDrop, print or another app through the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that is up to you and the service you choose.

4. Camera, photos and contacts

The camera is used only when you choose to take a job photo. Photo access is used only when you pick a logo or job photo. The contact picker lets you choose one contact to copy into a client; the app cannot read your address book otherwise. Nothing is uploaded.

5. Notifications

Overdue reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

6. Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction.

7. Children

Invoice Once is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
