Kept — Support

Need help with Kept?
Email: gefjonstudio@gmail.com
We usually reply within 2 business days.

Quick answers

  • My free space didn't go up: deleted items go to Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open it, tap Select, then Delete All to get the space back now.
  • iCloud Photos: with "Optimize iPhone Storage" on, deleting mainly frees iCloud space and a little on the phone.
  • Similar photos: Kept lights the best shot of each set (sharpest, favourites first) and marks the rest to clear. Tap another photo to move the light to it; long-press to look closer.
  • Swipe: right to keep, left to send a photo to the store room. Nothing is deleted until you clear the store room and confirm, and you can hang photos again from there: one, several or all of them.
  • Duplicate contacts: merging keeps the fullest card and adds every number and email from the others. A backup is saved first; undo any merge from Backups (top right).
  • Inbox Sweep: works with iCloud Mail, Gmail and Yahoo using an app password (Gmail needs 2-Step Verification on). Kept moves mail to your mailbox's Trash; your provider empties it later. It frees space in your email account, not on the iPhone.
  • Live Photos: converting keeps the still photo with its date and location and drops the motion clip.
  • Pricing: swiping is free forever; smart cleanup is free up to 500 MB a day; Kept Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription.
  • Restore a purchase: You tab > Restore Purchase.
  • Privacy: everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no servers.
