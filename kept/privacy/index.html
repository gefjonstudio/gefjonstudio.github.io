Kept — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Kept is a photo and video cleaner that works entirely on your iPhone. In short: your photos never leave your phone, and we collect nothing.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Kept has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

2. Your photos and videos

With your permission, Kept reads your photo library on your device to find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, large videos, Live Photos and blurry photos. All of this analysis happens on the device. No photo, video, thumbnail or analysis result is uploaded anywhere. Kept keeps a small cache of file sizes and image fingerprints inside its private storage so later scans are faster; deleting the app deletes it.

3. Contacts

If you open Duplicate Contacts and allow access, Kept reads your contacts on your device to find people saved more than once. Before any merge it saves a full backup (a vCard file) and an undo record inside the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded. You can share a backup yourself from the Backups screen.

4. Inbox Sweep (email)

If you connect an email account, Kept connects directly from your iPhone to your email provider's mail server (for example imap.gmail.com) using the app password you enter. The password is stored only in this iPhone's Keychain and is sent only to that mail server. Kept reads the sender, size, date and unsubscribe headers of messages in your inbox to group them by sender; it does not read or store message bodies, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Emails are only moved to your mailbox's Trash when you tap the button to do so. If you tap Unsubscribe, Kept opens or contacts the unsubscribe address the sender provided. Disconnecting removes the password from your iPhone.

5. Deleting and converting

Kept only deletes or changes a photo or video when you tap the button to do so, and iOS asks you to confirm every time. Deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos for 30 days. Converting a Live Photo saves a still copy with the same date and location and moves the original to Recently Deleted.

6. Purchases

The one-time Kept Pro unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Kept stores how much of the free cleanup allowance you have used in your device's keychain, which never leaves the device.

7. Children

Kept is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
