2. Free use and the one-time unlock

Swipe cleaning is free and unlimited. Smart cleanup (duplicates, similar photos, screenshots, screen recordings, large videos, blurry photos and Live Photo conversion) is free up to 500 MB per day. Finding duplicate contacts and seeing who fills your inbox are free; merging contacts and clearing or unsubscribing from email are part of Kept Pro. A single in-app purchase, Kept Pro, unlocks unlimited smart cleanup and contact merging. Undoing a merge is always free. It is a one-time purchase: there is no subscription and nothing renews. It is shared with your Family Sharing group and can be restored on your other devices.

3. Your choices

Kept suggests what to remove; you decide. Always review a selection before you confirm it. Deleted items stay in Photos' Recently Deleted album for 30 days, and you can recover them there.

4. What Kept does not do

Kept cleans photos and videos only. Like every App Store app, it cannot clear other apps' data, caches or iOS system data.
