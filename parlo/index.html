Yes. Download a language once in Settings, Languages and Parlo translates text, voice, conversations and camera with no internet, so it works on planes, subways and abroad without roaming.

No. Text translation and the phrasebook are free forever. Voice, conversation and camera can be tried 3 times a day free, and Parlo Pro unlocks them with a single $9.99 payment. Nothing renews.
