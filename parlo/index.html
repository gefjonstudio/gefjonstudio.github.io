Questions

Does Parlo work offline?

Yes. Download a language once in Settings, Languages and Parlo translates text, voice, conversations and camera with no internet, so it works on planes, subways and abroad without roaming.

Is Parlo a subscription?

No. Text translation and the phrasebook are free forever. Voice, conversation and camera can be tried 3 times a day free, and Parlo Pro unlocks them with a single $9.99 payment. Nothing renews.

Can Parlo translate a menu with the camera?

Yes. Point your iPhone at a menu or sign and every line is replaced in place with its translation. It also works on photos and screenshots, including vertical Japanese and Chinese.

Does Parlo collect my data?

No. Parlo uses Apple on-device translation. There is no account and nothing you type, say or photograph leaves your phone.

Which languages does Parlo support?

Arabic, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese.
