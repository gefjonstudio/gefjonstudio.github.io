Parlo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Parlo is built so that your words stay yours.

What we collect: nothing. Parlo has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run any servers that receive your data.

Translation: text is translated on your iPhone by Apple's built-in Translation framework. Language files are downloaded and managed by iOS, not by us.

Speech: when you use the microphone, your voice is turned into text by Apple's on-device speech recognition and is not sent to us. Translations are read aloud by the iPhone's built-in voices.

Camera and photos: images you capture or choose are read on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. They are not uploaded or stored by Parlo, except that a translation you keep appears in your on-device history.

History and saved phrases: stored only on your iPhone. You can delete items or clear history at any time in the app, and deleting the app removes everything.

Purchases: the optional Parlo Pro purchase is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children: Parlo does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes: if this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
