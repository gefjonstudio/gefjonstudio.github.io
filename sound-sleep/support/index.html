Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings (the gear) in Sound Sleep and tap "Restore Purchase". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy "Unlock Everything". It's a one-time purchase: there is no subscription to cancel.

Does the sound stop when my phone locks?

No. Sound Sleep is built to play all night with the screen off or locked. If sound stops, check that Low Power Mode or a Focus isn't closing apps, that the sleep timer isn't set, and that "Mix with other apps" is off if another app is taking over the audio.

How does the sleep timer work?

Tap the moon on the mix bar. Choose "Duration", "Stop at" a clock time (e.g. 6:30 am), or "All night". The mix fades out gently at the end; choose the fade length (1, 5, 10 or 20 minutes) in Settings → Timer fade-out.

How does "Wake me" work?

In the Timer sheet, turn on "Wake me" and choose a time. Sound Sleep fades your chosen morning sound in over 10 minutes so it reaches full volume at that time; after the sleep timer ends it waits silently. If you allow it, iOS also schedules a backup alarm (AlarmKit) in case the app was closed overnight. Leave Sound Sleep playing overnight (don't swipe it away) for the gentle wake.

What are Sleep Journeys?

Journeys (in the Mixes tab) change the sound over the night, for example from rain to deep brown noise, so it never feels repetitive.

How do I add the Control Centre button or Lock Screen widget?

Control Centre: swipe down from the top-right corner, tap +, then "Add a Control" and search for Sound Sleep. Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise → Lock Screen → add a widget and pick Sound Sleep. Both play or pause your current mix. You can also say "Play Sound Sleep" to Siri.

Do binaural beats need headphones?

Yes. Binaural beats work by playing a slightly different tone in each ear, so use stereo headphones. Isochronic tones, solfeggio frequencies and noise colours work on speakers too.

Can I play it alongside music or podcasts?

Yes: turn on Settings → "Mix with other apps".

It isn't loud enough.

Turn on Settings → Volume Boost (up to +6 dB, with a limiter so it never clips).

Is Sound Sleep a medical device?

No. Sound Sleep plays relaxing sounds. It can help mask background noise or ringing in the ears for some people, but it does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. Talk to a doctor about sleep problems or tinnitus.
