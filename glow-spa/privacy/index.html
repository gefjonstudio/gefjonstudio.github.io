Glow Spa — Privacy Policy

Glow Spa respects your privacy.

Data: Glow Spa collects no data at all. Your game progress, stars, purchases and settings stay only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

No ads, no tracking: Glow Spa has no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it never tracks you across other apps or websites.

Purchases: the optional Unlock Everything purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children: Glow Spa is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data: use Settings → Reset progress, or delete the app.
