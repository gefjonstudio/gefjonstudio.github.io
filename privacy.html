Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Gefjon Studio makes iPhone apps you pay for once. This policy covers this website, gefjonstudio.com. Each app also has its own privacy policy, linked below and from its App Store page.

The short version: no account, no cookies, no analytics, no ads. We do not ask you for any data on this website.

1. Our apps

Our apps do not require an account and do not collect, sell or track personal data. Content you create in an app stays on your device unless you choose to share it. Each app’s own privacy policy says exactly what that app does:

  • Kept: privacy policy
  • Parlo: privacy policy
  • Invoice Once: privacy policy
  • SignaturePro: privacy policy
  • Miscut: privacy policy
  • Sound Sleep: privacy policy

2. This website

This website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and it loads its fonts, images and sound samples from our own domain. The host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub’s own privacy policy. Links to the App Store and to your email app open services run by others, under their own policies.

3. Contacting us

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply to you.

4. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
