Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Gefjon Studio makes iPhone apps that work on your device. This policy covers our website, our social media accounts and the tools we use to publish posts to them. Each app also has its own privacy policy, linked from its App Store page.

1. Our apps

Our apps do not require an account and do not collect, sell or track personal data. Content you create in an app stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

2. This website

This website is a static page. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's own privacy policy.

3. Social media publishing

We use the official TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and YouTube APIs only to publish our own videos and images to our own accounts (@gefjonstudio). The access tokens are stored privately on our own computer and used for nothing else. We do not use these APIs to read, collect or store information about other people.

4. Contacting us

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply to you.

5. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
