1. What the app does

The app helps you measure trading-card centering and estimate condition and grade outcomes. All measurements, grade predictions, and value estimates are informational estimates only.

2. No guarantee of grading results

Predicted grades, sub-grades, centering tolerances and estimated values are generated from your photos and inputs using approximations of publicly described grading standards. They are not a guarantee of any result from any grading company, and actual graded values and sale prices vary with the market. Do not rely on the app as the sole basis for buying, selling, or submitting cards for grading.

4. On-device processing

Measuring, card identification and condition checks run on your device, and your photos and saved scans are stored there and are never uploaded. Miscut shows no ads, does no tracking and collects no data. It makes only anonymous requests for public data: a small file of newly released cards (about once a day) and, if Market prices is on, card prices from TCGdex and exchange rates from frankfurter.app. See the Privacy Policy for details.

5. Purchases

Measuring centering (front and back), saving scans and three free grade reveals each week are free. A single one-time unlock (a non-consumable In-App Purchase, priced in your local currency at purchase) unlocks grades on every scan, card names and card ID, the surface inspector, Brand Kit, market prices and the flip tracker. There is no subscription: you pay once and keep it.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase.
  • “Restore Purchases” in the app restores the unlock on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID.
  • If you previously paid for Miscut Pro (the monthly plan, the retired yearly plan or the retired lifetime unlock), you get the unlock automatically, with nothing to buy. Refunded purchases and free trials that never converted do not qualify. If your Pro subscription is still renewing, you can cancel it in your device Settings → your name → Subscriptions; we cannot cancel it for you, and it is no longer needed.
