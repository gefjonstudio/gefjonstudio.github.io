Liner notes: checking centering before you grade

  1. Scan the card. Lay the card flat and point the camera. Miscut auto-detects the edges, straightens and crops it with on-device vision, with a loupe for fine-tuning.
  2. Read both ratios. Centering is measured left/right and top/bottom, front and back, the same way the grading companies do.
  3. Compare against real standards. PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC, TAG and ACE each publish their own centering tolerances. The Standards tab lists them, and the unlock grades every scan against all six.
  4. Decide before you pay. Grading runs $15-25 a card whether it gems or not. Skip the submissions that were never going to hit the grade.

Questions

What does Miscut do?

Point your iPhone at any trading card and Miscut measures its centering: the left/right and top/bottom border ratios, front and back. You see whether a card can hit PSA 10, BGS 9.5 or SGC 10 centering before you pay to grade it.

Is Miscut a subscription?

No. Scanning and measuring are free. Unlock Everything is a single $5.99 payment that adds grades on every scan for PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC, TAG and ACE. Nothing renews.

How accurate is it?

The card is auto-detected, straightened and cropped with on-device computer vision, with a magnifier loupe and fine-tune dials. The grade thresholds use each company's published centering tolerances, listed in the app's Standards tab.

Are my card photos uploaded?

No. Scanning and measuring happen on your iPhone. Your card photos never leave your device.

Does Miscut have ads?

The free tier currently shows a small banner ad. Unlock Everything ($5.99 once) removes every ad, and an update that goes completely ad-free is on the way.

Which grading companies does it cover?

PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC, TAG and ACE, each with that company's real centering tolerances. It also identifies the card's name and set from an on-device card index.
