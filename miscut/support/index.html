Quick answers

The scan won't lock onto my card. Use even lighting without glare, a plain contrasting background, and hold the phone flat until the two crosses join. You can always drag the guide lines yourself — the magnifier makes pixel-perfect placement easy.

What does the ~ mark mean? Some cards (full-art and hyper rares) have no printed frame on one axis, so that ratio is an estimate — fine-tune it with the magnifier for an exact read.

Restore my unlock. More → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID. If you paid for Miscut Pro before, the unlock comes back automatically.

Cancel an old Pro subscription. It is no longer needed. iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Miscut Pro → Cancel (or More → Manage subscription). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Where do market prices come from? From TCGdex, for identified Pokémon cards, with exchange rates from frankfurter.app. The lookups are anonymous (only the card name and number, or a currency) and you can switch them off in More → Market prices.

Are there ads? No. Miscut has no ads, no tracking and collects no data. Free users see the grades for three scans a week; the one-time unlock (More → Unlock everything) shows them on every scan.
