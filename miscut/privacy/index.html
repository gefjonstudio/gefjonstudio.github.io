Miscut

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Miscut (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains exactly what stays on your device, what leaves it, when, and where it goes.

The short version. Miscut collects no data. It has no ads, no tracking, no accounts and no analytics. Measuring centering, identifying the card and checking its condition all happen on your device, and your card photos never leave it. Price lookups, exchange-rate lookups and the card-database download are anonymous requests that contain only a card name and number, a currency or a public file name, and never personal or device data.

What stays on your device

Card photos, measurements and saved scans. Every photo you take or import is analysed on your device. Your saved scans, measurements, card names, costs and prices are stored only on your device (and in your own iCloud or iTunes backups, which Apple manages under Apple’s Privacy Policy). Deleting the app removes all of it.

No ads and no tracking

Miscut has no advertising of any kind and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code from us or anyone else. It does not use your advertising identifier, does not ask to track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and shows no tracking prompt. Earlier versions (up to 2.0.2) showed third-party ads in the free version; version 2.1 removed them, together with the Google ad code, the consent message and the tracking prompt.

When the app uses the internet

Price lookups, exchange-rate lookups and the card-database download are anonymous requests that contain only a card name and number, a currency or a public file name, and never personal or device data. Nothing is sent to us, and the app attaches no account, cookie, device identifier or analytics header to any of them.

Card database updates. About once a day, when you open the app, it downloads a small public file of newly released cards from cards.cardlisterpro.com, so card names can be recognised. This is an ordinary download of the same public file for everyone: the request carries no account, no device identifier and nothing about you, your photos or your scans. As with any web download, the file’s host (Cloudflare) necessarily sees the connection’s IP address in order to deliver the file; we do not use it or link it to you.

Market prices. When Market prices is on (More → Market prices; you can switch it off at any time), the app asks TCGdex (api.tcgdex.net) for a price by sending only the identified card’s name and collector number. No photo, scan or personal or device data is sent, and the app keeps no cookies or cache for the request. Prices are shown only on your device.

Exchange rates. To show a price in your currency, the app downloads the public European Central Bank reference rates from frankfurter.app. The request names only a currency (EUR) and carries nothing about you or your cards.

As with any web request, TCGdex, frankfurter.app and Cloudflare (which delivers the card-database file) necessarily see the connection’s IP address in order to answer it; we do not receive, use or link it to you, and each service’s own policy covers what it does with it.

Purchases. The one-time unlock (and the optional tips) are sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we never see your payment details. The app reads your purchase history from Apple on your device, to restore the unlock and to recognise people who paid for Pro in earlier versions; none of it is sent to us.

Links you tap. The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use open in your browser.

Information we do NOT collect

  • No name, email, address, phone number or contacts
  • No location
  • No advertising identifier, and no analytics or usage tracking
  • No account or sign-in with us
  • No card photos, scans or measurements

What we hold on our servers

Nothing about your use of the app. Earlier versions of Miscut (before 2.0) could upload listing photos for a Whatnot Show Pack to an image host we operate, and could sign in to eBay through a relay that kept no tokens. Those features have been removed. Photos uploaded by earlier versions remain on the image host until you ask us to delete them.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and shows no ads. The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data retention & deletion

On-device data is yours to keep or delete; removing the app deletes it. To have photos uploaded by an earlier version deleted, email the address below and we will remove them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
