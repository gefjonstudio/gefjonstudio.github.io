Nordpuls Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email gefjonstudio@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Including your iPhone model, iOS version and the Nordpuls version (Settings, About) helps.

How do I start a workout?

Open the Train tab and tap Start. With an Apple Watch, the workout opens on your watch automatically.

I have no Apple Watch.

Connect any Bluetooth heart-rate strap in Settings, Heart rate from, or train with the timer and voice cues only.

How are my zones set?

Set your age or your known max heart rate at the top of Settings. Intervals target 85 to 95 percent of max, recovery 60 to 75 percent.

How does Nordpuls Plan decide?

After each plan session the summary shows the decision: move up, same level or step back. Two strong sessions in a row move you up; without a heart-rate sensor, three complete sessions do. A session you end before the first interval gets going is not counted.

How do I change the coach voice?

Settings, Coach, Voice. Tap the play button to preview each voice.

Heart rate is not showing.

Open Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, Nordpuls and turn on Heart Rate. On the watch, make sure the Nordpuls app is installed from the Watch app on your iPhone.

How do I restore Nordpuls Pro?

Settings, under The route: Restore purchase. It is also on the Nordpuls Plan page. It uses the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a session from the Progress tab, or delete the app. Nordpuls keeps nothing anywhere else. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.
